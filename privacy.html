Your Privacy Matters

JanPrahar News does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We are committed to being fully transparent about the minimal data we collect.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of information necessary to operate our service. This falls into two categories:

A. Information collected automatically (all visitors)

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URL
  • General geographic location (country/city, not precise location)
  • IP address (for fraud prevention and analytics)

B. Information you voluntarily provide (admin users only)

  • Email address (used for Firebase Authentication to access the admin dashboard)
  • Password (stored securely and encrypted by Firebase — we never see it in plaintext)
✅ Regular readers do not need to create an account. No personal data is collected from you beyond standard analytics.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Deliver news content and maintain website functionality
  • Understand how visitors interact with our content (analytics)
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • Authenticate admin users to manage the content management system
  • Detect and prevent abuse, spam, or security threats
  • Improve our website and Android application

We do not use your information for email marketing, profiling, or any purpose not listed above.

4. Cookies & Advertising

JanPrahar News uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

Cookie Type Provider Purpose
Essential Firebase Admin session authentication
Analytics Google Analytics Understanding site traffic and usage patterns
Advertising Google AdSense Displaying personalized or contextual advertisements
Preference JanPrahar News Remembering your dark/light mode preference

Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

Most browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this website.

5. Third-Party Services

JanPrahar News integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — database storage, authentication, and hosting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — advertisement delivery. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary — image upload and delivery for article images. Cloudinary Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — web fonts (Oswald, Source Sans 3). May log font requests with your IP address.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — CSS framework loaded via CDN.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party providers. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Admin passwords are managed entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never store or see plaintext passwords
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • Firebase Firestore security rules restrict write access to authenticated admin users only
  • Our codebase does not store sensitive user data in localStorage or cookies beyond session preferences

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

JanPrahar News is a general-audience news portal and is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at slok358@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at slok358@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
